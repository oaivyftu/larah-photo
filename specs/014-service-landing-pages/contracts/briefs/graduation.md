## The one risk of this page

Graduation has two projects today, and the demand for it is sharply seasonal. A
page that only says "congratulations" is the thinnest of the four. What a
graduate and their family need is practical: when to book around convocation,
whether the session is on campus, what to do about gowns and hoods, how it fits
between ceremonies. Those answers are the owner's. If she cannot supply them
yet, leave the page switched off until she can.

## Questions a client of this service wants answered

1. Who the session is for: university, college, high-school graduates; solo or
   with family and friends.
2. Whether the session is before, after, or separate from the ceremony, and why
   that matters for the gown and the schedule.
3. Where in London it can happen: on campus, a park, around the city.
4. How long it lasts and what the shots usually include (gown, cap, hood,
   diploma, with family, in casual clothes).
5. When to book: how far ahead of convocation, and how busy the season is.
6. What to wear and bring: the gown and hood, a change of clothes, shoes for
   walking.
7. What happens after: delivery time and form, including before announcements or
   gifts.
8. How to book it and what the starting price covers (from the package block).
9. What happens with weather, or a ceremony date that moves.
