## The one risk of this page

Portrait is the broadest term and the one with the most competition. "Portrait"
covers headshots, personal-brand sessions, creative portraits and couples. A page
that says "portraits" and nothing particular will read like every other. The
sections should say which kinds of portrait session the studio actually offers
and what its portraits look like. That is the owner's to say, so it is mostly
`[[CONFIRM]]`.

## Questions a client of this service wants answered

1. What kinds of portrait session the studio does (personal, professional,
   creative, couples, maternity — only those the owner confirms).
2. What the studio's portrait style is, in the owner's own words.
3. How a session runs: length, location, direction, how to feel at ease in front
   of the camera.
4. Where in London it can happen: studio, outdoors, at home or at work.
5. What to wear, what to bring, how to prepare hair and makeup.
6. How many photographs, how soon, in what form (digital, print).
7. How the photographs may be used (personal, professional, social).
8. How to book it and what the starting price covers (from the package block).
9. What happens with weather for an outdoor session.

- That the studio is the "best" or "most experienced" anything.
