## Both names, used naturally

Clients search "engagement" and "pre-wedding" interchangeably. The page must use
both in the **heading, introduction, at least one question and answer, search
title, search description and structured data** (SC-004). Use them the way a
client would say them. Do **not** invent a distinction between the two (they mean
the same session here), and do not stuff either word into sentences where it does
not belong.

## Questions a client of this service wants answered

The sections and the questions together should cover:

1. What an engagement or pre-wedding session actually is, and who it is for.
2. What the session is used for: save-the-dates, invitations, a wedding website,
   a guest-book or welcome sign, getting comfortable in front of a camera before
   the wedding day.
3. How the session runs: how long, how many locations, how directed it is.
4. Where in London it can happen, and how to choose a place.
5. When to book it relative to the wedding date, and the best seasons for it.
6. What to wear and what to bring (outfit changes, colours, comfort in the
   weather).
7. What happens after: when the photographs arrive and in what form.
8. How to book it and what the starting price covers (from the package block, not
   restated).
9. What happens if the weather turns.
